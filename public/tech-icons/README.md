Technology icons are stored locally and shared by the Skills section, technology marquee, and hero badges.

- Brand SVGs are from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), with the original colors and geometry. The MIT license is included in `LICENSE-devicon.txt`.
- The Apache Doris SVG is from its [official brand assets](https://doris.apache.org/brand-assets/). Its viewBox focuses on the symbol from the stacked logo; paths and colors are preserved. The Apache license is included in `LICENSE-apache.txt`.
- `sources.json` records the download URL for each file. Logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
- REST APIs use Lucide's network symbol because REST is an architectural style rather than a product brand.
